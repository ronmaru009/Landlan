Landlan Attends the 2nd Bangsamoro Housing and Human Settlements Forum

Landlan proudly participated in the 2nd Bangsamoro Housing and Human Settlements Forum organized by the BARMM Ministry of Human Settlements and Development (MHSD) last October 7–8, 2025. The two-day forum brought together experts and representatives from various institutions, including BARMM-MSSD, Catholic Relief Services, Sarmiento Group of Companies, Happy Living Realty and Development Corp., Amanah Bank, and many others. Each speaker shared valuable insights on land and community development, sustainability, energy efficiency, and financing mechanisms, such as Islamic banking, which are vital to the continuous growth of the BARMM Region.

The event proved to be highly productive and insightful. For Landlad it served as a meaningful opportunity to broaden our understanding of sustainable and inclusive housing development — enabling us to align our future projects with the region’s vision for a resilient, energy-efficient, and community-centered Bangsamoro.

Through this engagement, Landlan reaffirms its commitment to contribute to the region’s progress by integrating innovative, inclusive, and sustainable approaches in all our real estate and development initiatives.

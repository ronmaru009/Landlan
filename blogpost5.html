Landlan Explores Partnership with Sta. Lucia Marketing – Soccsksargen

Landlan visit Sta. Lucia Marketing – Soccsksargen, exploring a potential partnership that would allow us to feature their full portfolio of projects. Our team had the privilege of touring key developments, including Fairmont Lake Residences and Orchard Residences, and was thoroughly impressed by the quality and vision of their work. This visit marks an exciting step toward a collaboration that we believe will create immense value for our clients, providing them with access to some of the most sought-after properties in the region. We look forward to the possibility of a fruitful partnership as we continue to expand our offerings and deliver exceptional opportunities.

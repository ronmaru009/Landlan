Joining Economic Jihad Through Sustainable Housing Development

We are honored to be part of the Bangsamoro Autonomous Region in Muslim Mindanao’s (BARMM) continuing journey toward economic growth and development. With the recent announcement from the Bangsamoro Board of Investments (BBOI) that BARMM has already surpassed its annual investment target, we take pride in being one of the approved investors contributing to this milestone.
Our housing project in Cotabato stands as a reflection of our commitment to building not just homes, but communities that foster dignity, opportunity, and inclusive progress. By investing in sustainable and well-planned housing, we aim to support BARMM’s vision of a stronger and more prosperous region.We recognize that every investment is more than just figures—it is about creating impact. Through this project, we hope to generate employment, stimulate local industries, and help improve the quality of life for families in the Bangsamoro.We are grateful for the support of the BBOI and the leadership of BARMM, whose dedication to peace, good governance, and development has created a business environment where meaningful projects like ours can take root. This trust strengthens our resolve to continue being an active partner in the region’s transformation.Together with other stakeholders, we look forward to building not only houses but also a lasting legacy of progress for the Bangsamoro people.

About Us

Building Affordable Homes, Transforming Communities

Turning Land into Thriving Neighborhoods

We specialize in creating affordable, high-quality residential developments, helping Filipinos achieve their dream of homeownership.

  • Proven Track Record – Sold out our first 205-unit subdivision in record time.
  • Affordable Housing – Flexible payment options and strategic locations.
  • Community Impact – Supporting local jobs, growth, and sustainable living.

Founded in 2021, Landlan Realty and Development Corp. is driven by the mission to “create a land to liven an affordable nation.” From our first farm-lot project to large-scale residential communities, we remain committed to quality, affordability, and inclusivity—empowering families while contributing to local economic progress. With every project, we aim to build more than just houses; we create safe, vibrant, and well-connected communities where families can thrive, dreams can grow, and futures can be secured for generations to come. Together, we are shaping a better tomorrow for every Filipino family.

KC Homes Subdivision : Cotabato City's First Ever Housing Project

KC Homes is our pioneering residential development in Brgy. Tamontaka, Datu Odin Sinsuat, Maguindanao. As the first registered housing project in the area, it set the standard for affordable yet quality living—providing 205 families with a safe and thriving community to call home.

  • 01 Affordable Homeownership

    KC Homes introduced a flexible pre-selling model that allowed buyers to pay their down payments in easy installments, making quality housing accessible to more families.

  • 02 Prime Location

    Strategically located near Cotabato City, the subdivision benefits from the city’s rapid growth, including the rise of major establishments that create jobs and drive demand for housing.

  • 03 Community Growth

    Beyond building houses, KC Homes fosters a sense of belonging—offering families not just a place to live, but a community where they can grow and thrive together.

Frequently Asked Questions

  • How much is the required downpayment and how long can I pay it?

    Our standard downpayment for KC Homes is 10% of the total contract price, payable in 24 or 30 monthly installments, depending on your chosen payment term.

  • Can I use PAGIBIG membership for the housing loan in [KC Homes]?

    Yes. Once your downpayment is fully paid, we will process your PAGIBIG loan takeout, allowing you to finance the remaining balance through PAGIBIG.

  • What documents do I need to reserve a unit in [KC Homes]?

    We require a valid ID, proof of income( latest payslip or Certificate of Employment), and PAGIBIG membership ID or number. You also have to fill out a form that our staff will provide you.

  • How ling will it take before my house is turned over?

    The estimated construction and turnover period is 24-30 months.

  • Are there additional costs aside from the downpayment and monthly amortization?

    Yes. You will also need to cover the processing fees, move-in fees, and utility connection charges. We will provide you with a detailed breakdown of these costs during the turnover process.

  • Can I pay my downpayment in advance?

    Absolutely. You can make advance or lump-sum payments at any time, which may help you get your PAGIBIG loan processed sooner.

KC HOMES subdivision is committed to creating a masterplanned socialized housing development with the primary objective of providing quality, yet affordable house and lot units. Our goal is to ensure a community where quality living, security, accessibility, and a tranquil lifestyle are paramount for residents and their families. Spanning 1.85 hectares, the development is situated on slightly rolling terrain site, offering great views of the surrounding environment. It is also conveniently located near Cotabato Awang Airport, Tamontaka port, MSU Graduate School, and numerous schools and commercial areas.

Location and Access: The KC Homes Subdivision is conveniently located at Barangay Tamontaka, in the city of Datu Odin Sinsuat, Maguindanao Province, BARMM
